02 WHO WE ARE

Engineers first.
Aviators always.

AeroBMSCE is the official aerospace, aeromodelling and unmanned systems innovation club of BMS College of Engineering, operating out of Propel Lab–3. Since 2012, we've brought together engineers from 10+ departments to design, build and test advanced aircraft, drones and autonomous systems.

We compete internationally, publish research, and prototype flight hardware end-to-end — from CAD and CFD to carbon-fiber layups and flight tests. No simulations for the sake of it: everything we build is meant to leave the ground.

// OUR VISION

To be a leading aerospace and unmanned systems research group — renowned for design excellence, innovation and competitive success — cultivating future leaders of the aerospace and drone industry, and leaving a legacy of engineering excellence that inspires students worldwide.
